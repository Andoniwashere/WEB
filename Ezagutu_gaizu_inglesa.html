Who are we?

The Gazteleku initiative started in 2026. It was created in Ermua to organize activities aimed at the town's youth. At first, it was limited to summer activities, but thanks to the great reception it received, they are now organized throughout the entire year. This organization is possible thanks to the instructors, as they are responsible for looking after the young people.

Inside the Gazteleku



What do we do?

The Gazteleku association organizes activities in the Basque Country and its surrounding areas. Our calendar is not limited to summer; rather, all kinds of activities are organized all year round. There are more outings during holiday seasons, though they also take place on weekends.

Camping [2026/08/20]



Who is it aimed at?

Our activities are aimed at young people aged 12–18. Since most of them are organized in the Ermua area, local youth usually participate. This does not mean only they can take part; anyone who speaks Basque or English can sign up. The summer camps organized in the summer are international, giving young people the opportunity to interact with other cultures. To make this possible, instructors with English qualifications assist them.
