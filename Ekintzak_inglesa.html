Five unforgettable days at ZumaErtz: Adventure and nature on the Zumaia coast

This summer we will live a unique experience at the ZumaErtz summer camp, a five-day camp in Zumaia, one of the most spectacular spots on the Basque coast. From the very moment we pitch our tents, we will know that these will be days filled with friendship, learning, and a direct connection with nature.

Exploring the Flysch and Itzurun Beach

One of the most memorable moments of the camp will be discovering the impressive Flysch cliffs. Walking alongside these thousand-year-old rock formations and listening to the instructors' explanations will make us feel like true explorers. In addition, we will go down to Itzurun beach, where we will enjoy the waves, beach games, and refreshing swims in the Cantabrian Sea.

Hiking trails and camp life

Beyond the coast, we will take advantage of Zumaia's green surroundings to go hiking, enjoying panoramic views where the green of the meadows meets the deep blue of the sea. Upon returning to the main ZumaErtz campsite, living together as a group will be the soul of the trip. Together we will organize daily chores, prepare meals, and share laughs that will greatly strengthen the group.

Magical nights under the stars

At dusk, the camp atmosphere will transform. Evenings around flashlights will be filled with music, group activities, mystery stories, and night games. Sleeping in a tent, listening to the distant sound of the sea and the wind through the trees, will undoubtedly be the best way to recharge our batteries for the next day.

It will be five intense days at ZumaErtz, leaving our backpacks full of memories, new friends, and the desire to enjoy the magic of Zumaia all over again.
